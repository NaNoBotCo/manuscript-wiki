## What this is

A **phrommachat** (พรหมชาติ; Lanna *phromchat*) is the **household fortune-book** —
the illustrated almanac that reads a life from its birth-year. Its core is the
**twelve-year animal cycle**: your year sets your fate-figure, your auspicious and
inauspicious days, your marriage compatibility, the reading of your dreams and
moles and the omens that bear on you. Where the [holasat](/a?s=entity:holasat)
almanac is the astrologer's working table, the phrommachat is the *popular* face
of the same science — the book a family actually kept.

## The catalogue gap

**The catalogue holds zero witnesses whose title names a phrommachat** — every
spelling checked (*phrommachat, phromchat, phrom chat*) returns nothing across the
6,959 manuscripts.

*Inference —* like [lersi](/a?s=entity:lersi), this is a *shape* of absence, not a
sign of unimportance. The phrommachat is one of the most reproduced divination
texts in the Thai world; its total absence here says the two academic sources
digitised so far simply did not hold one under that name, **not** that Lanna lacked
the genre. It sits near the top of the want-list: maximum thematic weight, zero
availability.

## Beyond the catalogue — the illustrated fate-book

*Tradition —* the following is background from the wider Thai tradition, **not**
from these manuscripts; treat specifics as orienting, not authoritative.

- **A picture-book of fate.** The classic phrommachat is heavily **illustrated** —
  the twelve zodiac animals, paired male/female fate-figures, tables of lucky
  directions and days. It is one of the genres where *the image is the text*: a
  bare title line captures almost nothing of what the page carries.
- **Syncretic roots.** The name reads as *Brahma-jāti* ("Brahmā's kinds/births"):
  the tradition folds Brahmanical astrology into a Buddhist frame, exactly the
  divination-becomes-practice seam this archive tracks under
  [astrology](/a?s=genre:astrology) and [magic & ritual](/a?s=genre:magic_ritual).
- **The popular register.** Where a horoscope ([holasat](/a?s=entity:holasat))
  needs a specialist, the phrommachat is meant to be *consulted at home* —
  compatibility before a marriage, a day chosen for a journey, a dream read the
  morning after. It is divination as everyday domestic technology.
