## What this is

A **yantra** — Lanna *nyan* (ยันต์) — is a **sacred diagram**: a grid of syllables,
numbers and figures that concentrates protective or auspicious power into a
drawable form. Where a [katha](/a?s=entity:katha) is the spell *spoken*, a nyan is
the spell *inscribed* — laid onto cloth, paper, metal foil or skin so that its
efficacy stays fixed in an object. The catalogue holds 74 witnesses whose titles
name a nyan, and they sit squarely in the wichaa core.

> **A note on the name.** The corpus writes this as *nyan*, not *yantra* — the same
> reason the entity was initially invisible to a naive search. Beware, too, that
> *nyan* also transliterates *ñāṇa* ("insight") in scholastic titles; the match
> here is pinned to the diagram sense (*nyan lae katha*, *long nyan*, standalone
> *nyan*) to keep the two apart.

## Spell and diagram are one object

The single clearest pattern is that the nyan almost never travels alone: the
dominant title-form is **"Nyan lae khatha"** — *yantra and spell* — over and over.
The drawn diagram and the spoken formula are two halves of one technology, bound
in the same volume, and to inscribe a nyan is often literally to *"long nyan"* —
lay the diagram down while reciting its katha. The titles name the varieties by
their medium and purpose: *Nyan tian luang* (the great candle-yantra), *Nyan nam
bo kaeo* (a water-yantra), *Nyan pha sahang* (a cloth-yantra), and bundles that
fold nyan together with *[holasat](/a?s=entity:holasat)*, *sainyasat* and *cumnum
tewada* (assembling the deities). This is the apotropaic heart of the
[saiyasat](/a?s=genre:magic_ritual) in its most concrete, material form.

## The shape of the collection — a paper-and-cloth technology

Like the horā almanac, the nyan is a **working object, not an archival one**, and
its support proves it: **67 mulberry-paper against just 7 palm-leaf.** You draw a
yantra; you do not inscribe it into a palm-leaf for the library. It is overwhelmingly
research-priority — **61 of 74 flagged (82%)** — and cuts across the wichaa genres,
sitting under [astrology](/a?s=genre:astrology) (37) as much as
[magic & ritual](/a?s=genre:magic_ritual) (24). It is barely dated (3 witnesses,
1925–1954) — late paper — and it clusters in **Nan (27) and Lampang
(23)**, the vernacular working temples, with Wat Sung Men absent entirely.

## In the digested textbooks — the lek-yant system

*Corpus —* quoted from the **contributed printed wichaa textbooks** (OCR-digested
into the `pages` table), distinct from the palm-leaf catalogue measured above.
Citations give slug and page. Where the catalogue shows the nyan as a *paper-and-
cloth object*, the textbooks show it as a **system of knowledge** with its own
teacher-lineage and its own internal argument about legitimacy.

- **Yantra is a taught lineage of its own.** A *wai khru* roll-call separates the
  masters by craft: *"ครูเลขแลครูยันต์ ครูว่านแลครูยา ครูตำหรับแลครูตำรา"* — "the
  teacher of numerals and the teacher of yantra, the teacher of *waan*-herbs and of
  medicine, the teacher of manuals and of formularies" (*compendium-of-ancient-katha*
  p14). The nyan is not a loose skill; it descends through a named *ครูยันต์*
  (kru-yan) beside the numeral- and herb-lineages.
- **"Lek-yant" — numerals and diagram are one script.** The textbooks name the
  practice *เลขยันต์* (*lek-yant*, "numeral-yantra") and treat it as a writing
  system spanning tongues: *"เลขยันต์พันอักษรต่างภาษา"* — "lek-yant of a thousand
  letters in many languages" (*compendium* p70), located *"ในระบบเลขยันต์ต่างๆ"* —
  "within the various lek-yant systems" — across *จาม, มอญ, ขอม* (Cham, Mon,
  [Khom](/a?s=entity:yantra)) scripts (*supreme-maha-mantra-katha* p7). This is the
  syllable-grid this catalogue's diagram pages render as line-art.
- **An internal argument about orthodoxy.** One textbook stages the tradition
  reflecting on itself: *"พระพุทธเจ้ามิได้ทรงแจกยันต์ หากแต่ทรงแจกพุทธมนต์ มิใช่
  มนตร์มาร"* — "the Buddha did not hand out yantra, but Buddha-mantra; and not
  demon-mantra" (*supreme-maha-mantra-katha* p3). The claim polices a boundary —
  legitimate *phutthamon* versus illegitimate *mon man* — which is itself evidence
  that yantra sits on a contested seam between canonical Buddhism and efficacious
  saiyasat, exactly the seam this archive is built to hold open without flattening.

*Inference —* the textbook framing deepens the catalogue reading: the nyan is not
only a *material* technology (paper, cloth, skin) but a *scribal* one — a script
with a pedagogy, a lineage, and a live debate about where it belongs. The diagram
pages in the image store are the artefacts of that script; describing them (below,
and in the vision track) is what makes the lek-yant system searchable rather than
merely pictured.

## Notes

- **Read it as one unit with the katha.** The nyan/katha pairing is the atom of
  applied saiyasat: diagram + formula, object + utterance. Any finer pass should
  keep them cross-referenced rather than splitting the drawn from the spoken.
- **The image is the source.** Because a yantra *is* a visual artifact, its value
  is only fully captured when the page is seen, not just catalogued — this is a
  corpus where digitised imagery matters more than the title line, and a natural
  priority for the image store.
- **Under-dated and under-imaged — a crawl target.** High priority-density and in
  active living-practitioner use, yet thinly represented in the digitized record:
  the nyan corpus is central to the wichaa, and any practitioner's yantra-book —
  historical or contemporary — is a primary source worth documenting.
