## What this is

**Suep cata** (สืบชะตา — "to prolong the fate") is the great **life-extension
ceremony** of the north: the rite that lengthens a threatened lifespan, renews
waning fortune and wards off a fate the horoscope has read as failing. It is one
of the most characteristic and still most widely performed of Lanna rituals, and
the catalogue holds 21 witnesses whose titles name it. Small in count, it is
outsized in importance — **20 of the 21 are flagged research-priority (95%), the
densest priority-share of any subject the scorer touches.**

## Where divination becomes action

Suep cata is the hinge at which reading fate turns into *changing* it. The titles
put the two operations side by side — *"Suep anyu, Poet cata"*: **open the
horoscope** (*poet cata*), then **extend the life** (*suep anyu*). The astrologer's
[holasat](/a?s=entity:holasat) diagnoses the crisis; the suep cata rite is the
intervention. This is the clearest single answer in the collection to *what the
saiyasat is for*: not merely to know the future but to act on it, to buy back time
against a bad reckoning.

And it works at **every scale**. The manuscripts prescribe suep cata for a single
person, for a monk alone (*Pap suep cata phikkhu ton diao*), for the rice
(*Suep cata khao*), and — strikingly — for the **whole town**: *Suep cata ban
mueang*, the collective life-extension of the muang itself. The same ritual logic
that renews one body renews the body politic.

## The shape of the collection

It is a **working ritual manual on paper** — 17 mulberry-paper against 4 palm-leaf
— barely dated (3 witnesses, 1902–1939) and late, exactly the profile of a text in
active use rather than archival storage. It clusters in **Nan (10) and Phayao (6)**,
scattered across small vernacular temples with no dominant library — the wichaa
survival pattern, the opposite of the [canon](/a?s=genre:buddhist_canonical)'s Wat
Sung Men gravity.

## Notes

- **It anchors the whole ritual kit.** Suep cata rarely stands alone: the bundles
  bind it to [su khwan](/a?s=entity:su_khwan) soul-calling, *sut thon* recitation,
  *[holasat](/a?s=entity:holasat)* and offering (*Suep cata khao, ... Kam sut thon*;
  *Kam su khwan nya, Kam suep cata*). To read one suep cata manuscript is to read a
  practitioner's entire working repertoire for a life-crisis.
- **The astrology/magic seam runs straight through it.** It sits under both
  [astrology](/a?s=genre:astrology) (8) and [magic & ritual](/a?s=genre:magic_ritual)
  (12) because it *is* both — diagnosis and cure in one rite. It is the best worked
  example of why those two genres cannot be cleanly separated.
- **A living tradition, thinly recorded — a prime crawl target.** Near-total
  priority-density and still performed today, yet under-represented in the digitized
  record: suep cata is exactly the material this archive should document first. Any
  active practitioner's suep cata text is a primary source of the first rank.
