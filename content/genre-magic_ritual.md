## What this is

**Saiyasat** is the broad Lanna science of efficacious action — *yan* (yantra),
*katha* (spoken formulae), the consecration (*pluk sek*) that turns an object or a
rite into something that works. It is the esoteric core this archive exists to
document, and a **research-priority** genre.

But read the titles in this collection before assuming "magic" means sorcery. The
catalogue's raw line for almost every one of these is *Custom / Ritual*, and the
titles bear that out: *Ongkalakkhana* (the consecration of a *kathin* robe),
*Kham thwai khao* (verses for offering rice), *Sut thon* (protective recitation),
*Kham wai that* (veneration of a relic), *Uppatet suep cata* (rite for extending a
life). This is overwhelmingly the **benedictory and consecratory** end of saiyasat —
the ritual custom that frames merit-making and monastic life — rather than the
aggressive charm-craft the word "magic" conjures. The two ends share a technology;
the collection happens to hold mostly the communal, liturgical half.

## The shape of the collection

Where [Astrology](/a?s=genre:astrology) is a Phayao-Lampang tradition, ritual is
emphatically a **Nan** one: Nan holds far more of this genre than any other muang,
with Phayao, Lampang and Phrae following. The deepest single holdings sit at Wat Si
Mongkhon (Kong) and Wat Pa Mueat. As with all the learned genres it is written in
Tham Lanna, in the Pali-and-Lan Na register.

Its physical support splits more evenly than astrology's. Folding paper still leads,
but **palm-leaf runs it close** — consistent with a genre whose consecration and
offering texts were copied into the same durable canonical format as the liturgy
they accompany, rather than kept only as loose working almanacs.

## Handling this material

This archive treats esoteric material descriptively, not as a manual. These articles
and the catalogue record the *existence, provenance and shape* of a rite — they do
not reproduce activation instructions, and some material a lineage would consider
*khong khru* (restricted teacher-knowledge) is described only at the level a public
finding-aid needs. Takedown and correction requests go to 530kings@proton.me.

## Notes

- **The bucket is coarse.** Nearly everything arrived under a single raw label,
  *Custom / Ritual*, so genuine distinctions — consecration vs. offering vs.
  protective vs. life-extension — are collapsed here. Splitting this into sub-genres
  is the obvious next taxonomy pass once more titles are read.
- Ritual and horā frequently share a volume; expect overlap with the astrology
  corpus and check the raw metadata when a title names both.
