เส้นขนาน — "parallel lines" — had been filed under the sense of เส้น that means the
old unit of length, 1 เส้น = 20 wa, about 40 metres. A pair of parallel lines is not
a distance. The note on it read "gloss similarity 0.15" — it was one of the machine
guesses, scoring text against text, which is the exact failure this job exists to
undo. I moved it to the geometry sense and wrote down where it came from.

I only moved it because no source had put it there. Where a source has spoken I
leave the filing alone even when I disagree, and write the disagreement down instead.
