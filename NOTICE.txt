The Lanna manuscript wiki — licences, in layers

This repository builds wichaa.net. Different parts of it come from different
places and cannot carry one licence between them. The layers are deliberate, and
they are the ones wichaa.net has been publishing at /llms.txt — this file writes
them down where a machine cloning the repository will find them.

The verbatim text of the licence named first is in LICENSE beside this file.

WHAT CARRIES ITS OWN TERMS

  Manuscript images, and the 240px thumbnails derived from them in data/thumbs/
      Rights remain with each manuscript's holding library. The record's source
      link names the institution and its terms. Nothing is relicensed here.

  Wikipedia text — the sectioned articles, the intros on place pages
      CC BY-SA 4.0, credited on the page with a link to the licence, as the
      licence requires. Share-alike applies to that text.

  Wikidata
      CC0 1.0 public domain dedication. Credited anyway.

  OpenStreetMap-derived facility and place data
      © OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database Licence 1.0 (share-alike)
      https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

  Museum comparanda — Cleveland Museum of Art, the Met, the Smithsonian
      CC0 objects, credited to the holding museum.

  Pali and its English — the ho trai texts
      Mahāsaṅgīti Tipiṭaka and Bhante Sujato's translations, via SuttaCentral
      bilara-data, CC0 1.0.

  Zhou Yi
      The canonical text, public domain, shown verbatim.

  Province outlines — data/th-provinces.json
      Natural Earth, ne_10m_admin_1_states_provinces. Public domain, credited
      on /w/geo anyway. Provenance in data/th-provinces.SOURCE.txt.

  Vendored libraries in vendor/
      Each keeps the licence it arrived with.
